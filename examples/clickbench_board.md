## Cold win from chunked (string-dict queries)
Chunked replaces the full ~313ms dict decompress with a single 16K-frame decompress. Visible on first-touch string-dict cold:
- **Q25** (valsort) cold 549 → **198 ms** (warm 3.3 ms, 98x).
- **Q00** 1017 → 495, **Q15** 655 → 461, **Q05/Q08/Q10** already tiny.
- Scan/join-bound queries (Q11/Q12/Q14/Q16/Q17) are unchanged — they're not dict-decompress-bound.

## Unchanged from the reads (not chunked)
Warm wins: Q33 1066x, Q05 269x, Q08 218x, Q15 208x, Q04 200x, Q10 103x, Q00 13x, Q25 98x.
Losers: scans/joins Q02/Q09/Q11/Q12/Q14/Q16/Q17/Q19/Q30 (+ Q20/Q21/Q22 cold>45s timeout).
Errors (Class-A planner gaps): Q18/Q24/Q26/Q28/Q34/Q35/Q39/Q42. ok=False on Q02/Q03/Q06/Q17/Q30 are float/date-repr compare artifacts to verify individually.
