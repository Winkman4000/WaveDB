## Reading it
- **Big warm wins** (materialized / sidecar-backed / value-sorted): Q15 360x, Q05 328x, Q08 268x, Q04 227x, Q10 98x, Q33 1794x, Q25 90x (the valsort fix: 40,007ms -> 3.6ms warm / 549ms cold).
- **Scan / join losers** (0.05-0.9x): Q11/Q12/Q14/Q16/Q17/Q19/Q30 and the join+topk Q20/Q21/Q22 (timed out cold >45s).
- **ok=False on Q02/Q03/Q06/Q17/Q30** are mostly float-precision (AVG) and date-repr comparison artifacts in the hash check, not confirmed wrong answers — to be verified individually, not assumed bugs.
- **Errors (planner gaps)**: Q18/Q24/Q26 (alias / ORDER-BY-non-projected), Q28/Q34/Q35/Q42 (Literal/Sub/TimestampTrunc nodes), Q39 (dtype). These are the Class-A cluster from docs/crosswalk.md.
- **TIMEOUT>45s**: Q20/Q21/Q22/Q23/Q27/Q31/Q32 — slow joins/scans whose cold run exceeds the kill-timeout.

## Note on chunked dict
These cold numbers are on the mono-dict cb25db. The chunked-dict format (now the encode default) does not change **warm** numbers (the dict caches after first touch); its win is **cold**: any first-touch string-dict decode drops by ~the full-dict-decompress term (measured ~265ms on SearchPhrase: Q25 cold 409->145ms on a chunked single-col segment). A chunked re-encode of cb25db (plus rebuilding its gd/cube sidecars) is the deploy step to make the cold column reflect chunked.
